The rules, upfront

Minimums

From 10 pieces per style — genuinely. Below 100 pcs a small-run rate applies; from 100 the published bands take over. Sampling is €50 per style and is refunded against your first bulk order.

Sampling — €50

A sample costs us far more than €50. A pattern maker drafts and grades it, a designer checks the tech pack, a sample tailor sews it on a machine that stops running bulk, and it flies to you by courier. Real cost is several hundred euros per style. We charge €50 because we would rather you hold the garment than read a promise — and the €50 is credited in full against your first bulk order. Extra sample round: €50.

Payment

50% at order · 40% before shipment · 10% after delivery inspection. Corporates and clubs: invoice 30 days up to €5k after credit check.

Included

Fabric, making, standard labels and hangtags, polybag, final inspection, DDP delivery NL/BE/DE, EU customs and duties.

Surcharges

Small-lot dyeing (below 300 m), extra colourways, air freight, rush sampling. Always itemised in the quote — never discovered on the invoice.

Tolerance & claims

±3% on quantity. AQL 2.5 inspection. Claims within 14 days, settled by our Dutch company.
