CATEGORIES

What we make

Five categories, cut to pack in our own house — the same lines that have produced for UK, US and Italian brands. From ten pieces upward.

Our product categories

Knitwear

T-shirts, polos, fleece, hoodies, sweatshirts — plus uniforms, sports kits and tracksuits, the category our production house started with.

Cotton woven & bottoms

Shirts, chinos and cotton trousers, cargo and 6-pocket styles, shorts, fleece joggers and track trousers, overalls and dungarees. Small-batch dyeing available with a surcharge below 300 m.

Denim

Jeans in 4- and 5-pocket, denim jackets, shirts, shorts, dungarees and overalls. 12 oz and lighter, rigid and stretch, on US Denim fabric with our own wash and finish. This is the category our floor is built around.

Workwear — where we are strongest OUR CATALOGUE

Hi-vis, industrial, flame-resistant, outdoor, medical and tactical. Coveralls and overalls, work shirts and trousers, hoodies and jumpers, jackets, coats and vests, rain gear, scrubs and lab coats. Taped hi-vis to order, custom logo and colour on every style. See the full workwear range and catalogue →

Towels & terry

Bath sheets, bath and hand towels, face cloths, gym and sports towels, and hotel or spa sets. Cotton terry from roughly 400 to 600 gsm, combed or ring-spun, in plain, dobby border or jacquard, with woven labels or embroidered logos. Piece-dyed to your colour, hemmed or with a cam border. €2–6 a piece across the range, depending on size, weight and cotton quality.

Leather

Jackets, bags and accessories cut and stitched in our own leather section — not bought in from a third workshop. Cowhide, sheepskin (nappa and suede), goat and buffalo, in full-grain, top-grain and finished leathers, with the temper and thickness chosen per style. Small runs welcome, since leather rewards hand-work more than volume.

The workwear range

Our production house specialises in workwear and supplies it to companies in Europe, the US and locally. Everything below is made to your logo, your colours and your size grading — these are the shapes, not a fixed catalogue.

Hi-vis

Taped coveralls and overalls, action-back overalls, cargo and track trousers, mesh polos, utility shirts, holster shorts, fleece hoodies, quarter-zip jumpers, 5-in-1 rain jackets, long rain coats and vests.

POLY/COTTON TWILL 270 GSM · PRESHRUNK TWILL 310 GSM · FLEECE 300 GSM · MESH 140 GSM

Industrial

Coveralls and overalls, work shirts and trousers, hoodies and jumpers, jackets, coats and vests. Cotton twill, poly-cotton and cotton-spandex poplin, in the weight the job needs.

COTTON TWILL 210–245 GSM · 98/2 COTTON-SPANDEX POPLIN

Flame resistant

FR coveralls and overalls, FR shirts, FR trousers and FR jackets, built for work where the garment is the last line of protection.

CERTIFIED FR FABRICS TO SPECIFICATION

Outdoor & rain

Rain shirts, rain trousers, rain coats and jackets, and full rain suits — PU-coated oxford, taped seams, cut for movement.

POLYESTER OXFORD PU 150D 110 GSM

Medical

Scrubs and lab coats, in the fabric weights and colours a hospital or clinic specifies, with embroidery or printed identification.

POLY-COTTON · CUSTOM COLOURS

Military & tactical

Security and forces uniforms, personal uniforms and special combat trousers, made to the specification supplied.

TO CUSTOMER SPECIFICATION

Every workwear style is customisable — design, gender, colour, logo, size, fabric weight, printing and embroidery. Reflective taping, heat-seal, screen print and embroidery are done in-house. Workwear customisation starts at 50 pieces per style; the ten-piece capsule tier applies to our knit, woven and denim ranges.
