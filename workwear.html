TO CUSTOMER SPECIFICATION

Every style is customisable — design, gender, colour, logo, size, fabric weight, printing and embroidery. These are the shapes we build most often, not a closed catalogue: if your specification is different, send it and we will quote it.

Fabric is never your problem

You do not need a mill, a fabric agent or a minimum order of cloth. Tell us what the garment has to do — the weather, the shift length, the washing regime, the standard it must meet — and we source to that requirement.

Buying, dyeing, lab dips, shrinkage testing, labelling, printing, embroidery, packing and logistics all sit on our side of the line. Bring a swatch or a garment you already like and we will match it. Bring nothing and we will put options in front of you with a price per piece against each.

How a workwear order runs

01

Specification

Tell us the job the garment does and the standard it must meet. We come back with fabric options, a price per piece and a delivery date — within 48 hours.

02

Sample

One garment in your fabric, your colour, your logo, in 2–4 weeks for €50 — credited against your first order. Wear it, wash it, then decide.

03

Production

Cut, sewn, taped, embroidered and packed on our own lines. You see the order move through — no six weeks of silence.

04

Delivered

To your door, customs cleared and duties paid, on one European invoice in euros. You are not the importer.
