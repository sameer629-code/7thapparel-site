What our European desk takes off yours

The sewing is rarely what stops a brand buying from Asia. It is everything wrapped around it — the bank guarantees, the freight, the customs entry, and not knowing who you are really dealing with. Ordering through our European company removes all of it, and you still get the factory rather than a middleman's markup.

01

No letter of credit

An LC ties up your cash for months, costs bank fees at both ends, and dies on a typo. You are buying from a Dutch company, so you get an invoice on agreed terms — 50/40/10, with the last 10% held until you have inspected the goods.

02

A company you can look up

You order from a registered European business with a public company number, a real address and people you can meet — not an inbox that answers at three in the morning. If something needs sorting out, it gets sorted out here.

03

We are the importer, not you

DDP to your door. Export documents, freight, customs clearance, duty and import VAT are ours to handle. No EORI to arrange, no broker to appoint, no container sitting in Rotterdam waiting for a signature.

04

Paid in euros, like any supplier

One EUR invoice, one bank transfer inside Europe. No currency exposure, no correspondent-bank delays, no payment disappearing through three intermediaries on its way to Asia.

05

Same group, not a broker

The European company and the factory have the same owners. Nobody is adding an agent's margin between your tech pack and the cutting table, and nobody can lose your file between two firms.

—

What you still get

Direct access to the floor. Ask for a line photo, a fabric swatch, an inline measurement — it comes from the people making your order, not from a sales desk relaying a question.

WHY IT MATTERS AT YOUR END

Order small. Move fast when it works.

Most brands lose money twice on the same mistake — once by over-ordering a style that never sells, and once by under-ordering the one that does and then waiting four months for more. A floor this size, owned by us rather than rented by the order, is what fixes both.

THE ARGUMENT

Don't bet the season on a guess

Start at ten pieces. Test the fit, the fabric, the price on your own customers. Commit real money only to what has already sold — and keep your cash in the business instead of in a warehouse.

SPARE CAPACITY

If it hits, we can chase it

We deliberately hold headroom on the lines. When a style takes off, a repeat run starts within days rather than queueing behind somebody else's programme — because it is our floor, and we decide what goes on it.

THE FLOOR

Roughly 1,500 m², hundreds of machines

Three units, three production lines, and a machine park deep enough to run knit, woven, denim, workwear and leather at the same time. 50,000–100,000 pieces a month, and it scales into the millions over a year.

You can watch it being made

The usual experience of offshore manufacturing is silence for six weeks and then a container. We run the floor on a digital tracking system, and we open it to you — because the fastest way to earn trust is to stop asking for it.

01

Live order status

See where your order actually is — fabric in, cutting, sewing, finishing, packing — instead of waiting for someone to answer an email about it.

02

Tagged at finishing

Units are tagged as they come off finishing, so the count you are told is the count that physically exists. No rounding, no optimistic estimates.

03

Photographed, not described

Photo and measurement data captured against the order, so you can check the real garment against your spec while there is still time to change something.

04

Problems surface early

If a fabric lot is late or a measurement drifts, you hear it that week — not on the day the shipment was supposed to land.
