OUR PRODUCTION HOUSE

Saee Kamil — ours, not a supplier.

7th Apparel is the European sister company of Saee e Kamil (Pvt) Ltd, our own production house in Pakistan — making garments since 2011, incorporated as a private limited company in October 2020 — knitwear first, a dedicated denim unit since May 2021. Cutting, sewing, quality control, finishing and packing happen in-house; specialist decoration and denim washing run through long-standing local partners under our QC. No brokers — one accountable partner.

How the two homes work together

Amsterdam, Netherlands

Your contract, your invoice, your account manager. Quotes, sampling coordination, quality gate, DDP import and delivery, after-sales — all handled from our European desk.

The making: pattern, cutting, sewing lines, inline and final QC, finishing, packing, export. One accountable team from thread to carton.

* Sustained output on standard programmes; peak capacity with extended shifts and our hourly operator pool, product-dependent. Team: 35 permanent staff plus 15–65 trained hourly operators by season — 50–100 people on the floor at peak. Factory visits welcome — or we bring the production house to you: video walk-through plus a physical sample box.
